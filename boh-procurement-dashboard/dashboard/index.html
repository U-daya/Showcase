BOH Equipment Procurement

A dashboard for estimators: how equipment prices are moving, when bulk orders get cheaper, which vendors deliver on time, and how much lead time to plan for.

Built on sample purchase-order data I generated to match the shape of a real procurement export. No real company data is used.

What this tells an estimator

    Price trend

    Median unit price index for 1 to 2 unit orders (Q1 2024 = 100). Small orders only, so bulk discounts don't hide real price changes.

    Bulk-order thresholds

    Median discount per unit compared with 1 to 2 unit orders in the same category and year.

    Price change by category

    How much small-order prices rose from Q1 2024 to Q2 2026. Click a bar to filter the whole dashboard to that category.

    Vendor reliability

    Each vendor plotted by price against on-time delivery. Top left is the best place to be: cheaper and reliable.

    Lead times: quoted vs actual

    Days from order to delivery. The blue bar is the typical (median) order. The amber segment is the extra time between the vendor's quote and the slowest 10% of orders (p90): the buffer a schedule needs.
