BOH Equipment Procurement

A dashboard for estimators: how equipment prices are moving, when bulk orders get cheaper, which vendors deliver on time, and how much lead time to plan for.

Built on sample purchase-order data I generated to match the shape of a real procurement export. No real company data is used.

What this tells an estimator

    Price trend · 1 to 2 unit orders

    Median unit price index, Q1 2024 = 100. Small orders only, so bulk discounts don't hide real price changes.

    Bulk-order thresholds

    Median discount per unit compared with 1 to 2 unit orders in the same category and year.

    Price change by category · Q1 2024 to Q2 2026

    Change in the small-order price index. Click a bar to filter the whole dashboard to that category, click it again to go back to all equipment.

    Vendor reliability

    Each vendor by price against on-time delivery. Top left is the best place to be: cheaper and reliable. Dot size shows spend. Hover a table row to find its dot.

    Reliable (90% or more) Watch (80 to 90%) At risk (under 80%)
    Lead times · quoted vs actual

    Days from order to delivery, sorted by the slowest 10% (p90). The amber segment runs from the vendor's quote to the p90: the buffer a schedule needs.
